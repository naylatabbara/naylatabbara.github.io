A little about me

  • Born and raised in Beirut, Lebanon, I began my journey in architecture, where I developed an appreciation for structure, visual composition, and the way people experience spaces. Over time, my interests expanded toward branding, storytelling, and digital design, leading me to Milan to pursue a Master's degree in Communication Design at Politecnico di Milano.
  • Today, I bring my architectural perspective into brand identity, UX/UI, and web design, combining visual creativity with a user-centered approach to create thoughtful, functional, and engaging experiences.
  • Outside of design, you'll usually find me traveling, going on road trips, spending time with family and friends, or discovering inspiration in everyday things.
  • I speak Arabic, French, and English, and Italian at a B1 level.
